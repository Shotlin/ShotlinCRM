# Shotlin CRM page mockup prompts

## Shared art direction

Use case: polished high-fidelity desktop SaaS CRM UI reference mockup.
Composition: straight-on full-screen 16:10 application view; slim solid-black left navigation rail; white content canvas; 28px outer gutters; 20px card padding; subtle 10px radii.
Style: premium modern minimalism; Inter-like typography; near-black headings; cool-gray metadata; precise 1px light-gray dividers; realistic concise CRM data; sparse muted status colors; excellent alignment and whitespace.
Brand text: “Shotlin CRM”.
Constraints: no browser frame, gradients, glass effects, decorative 3D, watermark, lorem ipsum, oversized illustration, or saturated colored background. Add the specified small uppercase letter-spaced page label in the upper-left image corner.

## 01 — Overview

Label: “01 / OVERVIEW”. Header “Good morning, Sayan”, date, and New lead button. Four KPI cards: new leads, active pipeline, payments due, this-month revenue. Compact six-stage pipeline, restrained monthly received-revenue chart, recent Meta/WhatsApp leads, and upcoming follow-ups.

## 02 — Pipeline

Label: “02 / PIPELINE”. Source/owner filters, Kanban/List toggle, New lead button. Six columns: New lead, Connected, Proposal sent, Accepted, Payment due, Payment received. Cards show name, short enquiry, source, value, and follow-up. Include column totals and one subtly elevated dragged card.

## 03 — Inbox

Label: “03 / INBOX”. Three-pane layout: searchable conversation list, central brand-neutral WhatsApp-style thread, right lead context. Show name, initial enquiry, timestamps, stage, value, and next follow-up. Anchor composer; label workspace “Demo conversations”.

## 04 — Lead detail

Label: “04 / LEAD DETAIL”. Lead identity, WhatsApp source, Connected status, value, Edit lead. Left conversation/notes; right chronological proposal, status, invoice, and payment timeline. Next-follow-up card plus Create proposal and Move to stage actions.

## 05 — Clients

Label: “05 / CLIENTS”. Search, payment filter, Add client, compact summary strip. Clean table: client, company, contact, active proposals, received value, outstanding balance, last activity. Five realistic mixed-currency clients and restrained badges.

## 06 — Client detail

Label: “06 / CLIENT DETAIL”. Client identity/contact with received and outstanding values. Tabs: Overview, Proposals, Invoices, Activity. Show active engagement, recent conversation, upcoming follow-up, and related-record tables with editorial spacing.

## 07 — Proposals

Label: “07 / PROPOSALS”. Status tabs and New proposal. Register: number, title, client, amount/currency, status, validity, last update. Include draft, sent, accepted, and expiring-soon examples plus sent/accepted value summaries.

## 08 — Proposal editor

Label: “08 / PROPOSAL EDITOR”. Split editor and live summary. Left: client, title, validity, currency, line items. Right: polished proposal summary with subtotal/total. Save draft and Mark as sent; no fake PDF chrome.

## 09 — Invoices and payments

Label: “09 / INVOICES & PAYMENTS”. KPIs: outstanding, overdue, received this month, paid invoices. Invoices/Payments tabs. Table: number, client, issue/due dates, original amount, balance, status. Include partial/full examples and Record payment action.

## 10 — Invoice editor

Label: “10 / INVOICE EDITOR”. Split invoice editor/live preview. Left: client, currency, dates, line items, payment notes. Right: white invoice preview with number, client, itemized totals, amount due, and status. Include Record payment.

## 11 — Finance

Label: “11 / FINANCE”. Reporting-currency selector and Sample FX label. KPIs: This month revenue, Last quarter revenue, Outstanding, Overdue. Monthly received-revenue chart, currency/source breakdown, recent payments, and compact previous-period comparison.

## 12 — Calendar

Label: “12 / CALENDAR”. Month/Week toggle with Week selected. Seven-column schedule, slim time gutter, current-day emphasis, sparse follow-up/meeting/proposal-expiry/payment-due events. Right upcoming agenda with related lead/client names.
